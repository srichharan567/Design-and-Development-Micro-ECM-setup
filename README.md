# Design and Development of a Micro-ECM Setup

## About the Project

This project was carried out as part of my mechanical engineering internship at the Indian Institute of Technology Dharwad (IIT Dharwad), under the guidance of Prof. Vyom Sharma.

The project focuses on the design and development of a Micro-Electrochemical Machining (Micro-ECM) setup, including 3D CAD modelling, component assembly, and understanding the working principles of the machining system.

## Internship Details

- **Institute:** Indian Institute of Technology Dharwad
- **Department:** Mechanical, Materials and Aerospace Engineering
- **Project Supervisor:** Prof. Vyom Sharma
- **Student Department:** Mechanical Engineering, NIT Kurukshetra

## Objectives

- To understand the working principle of Micro-Electrochemical Machining.
- To develop a 3D CAD model of the setup using SolidWorks.
- To understand the functions and arrangement of the major components.
- To participate in the assembly of the available setup components.
- To study the electrolyte circulation system and tool–workpiece arrangement.

## Work Carried Out

- Developed a 3D CAD model of the Micro-ECM setup using SolidWorks.
- Worked on assembling the available components of the setup.
- Studied the CNC router-based tool positioning mechanism.
- Identified the functions of the DC power supply, electrolyte tank, pump, pipes, tool electrode, and workpiece holder.
- Prepared schematic representations for technical documentation.

## Major Components

| Component | Function |
|---|---|
| CNC Router | Provides tool positioning and movement |
| DC Power Supply | Supplies electrical potential for machining |
| Electrolyte Tank | Stores the electrolyte |
| Pump | Circulates the electrolyte |
| Tool Electrode | Enables electrochemical material removal |
| Workpiece Holder | Supports the workpiece |
| Pipes and Connectors | Facilitate electrolyte circulation |
